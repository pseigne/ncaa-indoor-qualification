# NCAA qualification monitor

Saved NCAA Division I qualifying lists and their recorded performances.

## Language

**Qualification cutoff**:
The required position in a saved qualifying list and the performance recorded at that position, when present. A qualification cutoff does not confirm championship entry, which can differ after declarations and scratches.

**Field line**:
The deepest position in a saved qualifying list that the championship field reached, from the NCAA's accepted list on declaration day. Athletes inside the qualification cutoff may be missing from the field after they scratch or don't declare, so the field can reach past the cutoff. Later scratches and replacements aren't reflected.

**Missing cutoff**:
A saved roster without the required qualification cutoff position. Its last available ranking is not a replacement cutoff.
